📱 Mobile Sales Analysis Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

This Power BI project analyzes mobile sales data to understand sales performance, customer ratings, popular mobile brands, and payment methods.

🛠️ Tools Used

- Power BI
- DAX
- Data Visualization

📂 Dataset

The dataset includes:

- Brand
- Mobile Model
- City
- Customer Name
- Customer Age
- Customer Ratings
- Date
- Payment Method
- Price Per Unit
- Total Quantity
- Total Transactions
- Total Sales

📊 Dashboard Features

- KPI Cards: Total Sales, Total Quantity, Total Transactions, and Average Price.
- Monthly Analysis: Total quantity sold by month.
- Brand Analysis: Sales comparison by mobile model.
- City Analysis: City-wise mobile sales using a map.
- Customer Ratings: Distribution of customer ratings.
- Payment Analysis: Sales breakdown by payment method.
- Interactive Filters: Month, brand, city, payment method, and customer name.

💡 Key Insights

- Total sales are approximately 769M.
- Total quantity sold is approximately 19K.
- Total transactions are approximately 4K.
- Average price is approximately 40K.
- The dashboard helps compare monthly sales, mobile models, cities, customer ratings, and payment preferences.
